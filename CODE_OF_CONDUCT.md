# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in nodecheck a welcoming and respectful
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include being
kind, patient, constructive, and respectful of differing viewpoints. Examples
of unacceptable behavior include harassment, insults, personal attacks,
unwelcome sexual attention, and publishing another person's private
information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned
with this Code of Conduct. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the
maintainers listed in the repository's GitHub contact information. All reports
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant][covenant],
version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
